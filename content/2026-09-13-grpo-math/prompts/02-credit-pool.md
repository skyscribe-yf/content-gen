## Prompt

Flat vector editorial illustration, warm cream background (#F8FAFC), amber-orange and deep-blue palette, no text, no numbers, no letters. A single glowing golden coin-stamped score seal at the top tilts and pours one stream of molten gold onto an extremely long unrolled paper scroll that stretches far into the distance, densely covered with tiny abstract ink marks. The gold spreads uniformly and thinly over every mark on the entire scroll, so each mark receives only a faint identical wash — near the pour point and at the far end the coating is equally thin. Small red flag markers at scattered spots on the scroll get covered by the same gold as everything else. Minimalist, generous negative space, soft shadows, notion-style editorial. Strictly no characters, no digits, no words anywhere.
