词嵌入入门 - 封面图

Cinematic widescreen conceptual cover image. 2.35:1 aspect ratio.

A warm cream paper background with a large traditional Chinese medicine cabinet (中药柜) in the center, drawn in a hand-drawn sketch style with slight wobble. The cabinet has many small drawers arranged in a grid, each drawer glowing with soft pastel light (Light Blue #A8D8EA, Mint Green #B5E5CF, Lavender #D5C6E0, Peach #FFD5C2) as if containing hidden coordinates. From one open drawer, a stream of floating numbers and coordinates flows out into a luminous high-dimensional space — suggesting "text becomes coordinates." 

The title text "词嵌入入门" is hand-lettered in large bold black ink at the top, slightly wobbly, with a small underline doodle.

Below the cabinet, a subtle visual metaphor: a single Chinese character "猫" transforming into a string of decimal numbers [0.012, -0.034, 0.078, ...], showing the text-to-vector mapping.

PALETTE: macaron — soft pastel blocks on warm cream
COLORS: Warm Cream background (#F5F0E8); Black (#1A1A1A) for lines and text; Light Blue (#A8D8EA), Mint Green (#B5E5CF), Lavender (#D5C6E0), Peach (#FFD5C2) for drawer glows and coordinate streams.

STYLE: Hand-drawn educational style with slight wobble on all lines. Airy, minimal composition with 40-60% whitespace. No realistic or photographic elements. Diagram-style visuals only.

MOOD: Balanced — warm, inviting, educational. Not dramatic or dark.

TEXT: Title "词嵌入入门" only, hand-lettered in black ink, large and prominent. All text in Chinese.
