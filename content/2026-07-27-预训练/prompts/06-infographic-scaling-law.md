Scaling Law - Loss vs Compute Power Law Curve

Scientific infographic showing the empirical scaling law relationship between model compute and loss.

LAYOUT: Scatter plot with curve fitting, plus explanatory annotations.

MAIN PLOT AREA: Log-log scatter plot
- X-axis: "计算量 (FLOPs)" — logarithmic scale, labeled with values from 10^20 to 10^25
- Y-axis: "验证集 Loss" — logarithmic scale, labeled from 1.0 to 4.0
- Data points: 8-10 scattered dots showing decreasing trend
- Fitted line: Smooth downward curve (power law) passing through points
- Trend arrow: Downward-pointing arrow along the curve labeled "Loss 持续下降"

MODEL LABELS near data points:
- "GPT-2" at lower-left
- "GPT-3" at mid-range
- "GPT-4" at upper-mid
- "DeepSeek V4" at the compute frontier
- Question mark "?" at right edge beyond current data — future models

KEY ANNOTATION: Arrow pointing right with caption "目前还没到平坦区" (Haven't hit the flat zone yet). Shaded region to the right of current data labeled "未知区域".

FORMULA BOX: Bottom-right corner showing the scaling law equation in a rounded rectangle.
