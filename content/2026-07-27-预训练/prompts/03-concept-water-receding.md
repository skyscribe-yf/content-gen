Water Receding Metaphor - Pretraining Stages

Conceptual illustration showing water gradually receding to reveal deeper and more complex structures, as a metaphor for pretraining progress.

LAYOUT: Vertical cross-section view with four water levels, left to right.

LEFTMOST (highest water ~ppl 100): Completely submerged landscape. Only random ripples visible on surface. Label: "随机初始化 — 完全混乱"

SECOND (~ppl 15): Water recedes slightly, revealing basic rock formations. Simple geometric shapes emerge. Label: "字频学习 — 高频字浮现"

THIRD (~ppl 5): More water recedes, revealing structured terrain — small buildings, pathways, recognizable shapes. Names of characters appear carved into stone. Label: "语法搭配 — 名字与短语"

RIGHTMOST (~ppl 2 lowest water): Full landscape revealed — complex city with interconnected structures, bridges, networks. All detailed and organized. Label: "知识浮现 — 长程依赖"

WATER LINE: Shown as a descending stepped line from left to right with ppl values at each step. Arrow pointing down labeled "训练步数增加".

COLORS: Deep Ocean Blue (#1A3A5C) for water, Golden Yellow (#F6AD55) for exposed structures, Terracotta (#C05621) for deepest layer details, Soft Peach (#FFECD2) for sky, Warm Orange (#ED8936) for mid-stage elements

STYLE: Clean vector illustration, isometric cross-section view, bold Chinese labels, clear layering
