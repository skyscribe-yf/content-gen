# Cover Image Prompt

A cinematic 21:9 widescreen cover image. Dark, contemplative atmosphere.

**Central metaphor**: A lone figure sitting on a long wooden bench in a vast, dimly lit corridor that stretches into the distance. The bench is cold and worn — a "冷板凳" (cold bench). At the far end of the corridor, a door is slightly ajar, letting in a single beam of warm golden light — the "门径" (door to insight). The figure is not looking at the door yet — they are looking down, deep in thought, with mathematical symbols and equations faintly floating in the air around their head like fireflies.

**Visual elements**:
- The bench occupies the lower third, stretching from foreground to middle distance
- The corridor walls are lined with subtle mathematical notation (integrals, primes, Greek letters) etched like ancient inscriptions
- The single beam of light from the slightly open door creates a dramatic focal point
- Small glowing particles (like fireflies) representing half-formed thoughts
- Color palette: deep navy blue (#0d1b2a) to charcoal (#1b2838) background, warm golden (#d4a855) light beam, cool cyan (#4cc9f0) for the floating symbols
- The mood is quiet perseverance, not despair — the light is there, the figure just hasn't looked up yet

**Title text**: "数学太难？" in clean sans-serif Chinese characters, positioned in the upper left area with generous whitespace. The text should be white with a subtle glow, legible but not dominant.

**Composition**: 40-50% breathing room. The figure is small relative to the vast corridor — emphasizing the loneliness of the cold bench. The warm light at the end provides hope without being saccharine.

**Style**: Digital illustration with a painterly quality. Not photorealistic. Think concept art for a contemplative indie film. The mathematical symbols should feel organic, not technical — like they're part of the atmosphere rather than a textbook.

**NO realistic human faces** — the figure is a silhouette or seen from behind. No text other than the title.
