# Cover Image Prompt: 注意力机制

## Title
注意力机制是什么？别再当数据库查询

## Dimensions
- **Type**: conceptual + metaphor (对比式概念图)
- **Palette**: dark (深色底，暖色科技感)
- **Rendering**: digital (3D渲染风格，高质量)
- **Text**: title-only
- **Mood**: balanced
- **Aspect**: 2.35:1 (电影宽幅)
- **Language**: zh

## Prompt

A cinematic 2.35:1 wide-format cover image for an AI/tech article. Dark background with deep navy-to-black gradient.

**Central visual composition — a striking "vs" contrast between two halves:**

**LEFT HALF (the misconception — database query, crossed out):**
A cold, rigid, mechanical server rack or database storage tower made of metallic blue-gray rectangles stacked in strict columns. The structure looks lifeless and binary. A large glowing red "×" or diagonal slash crosses through this entire left half. The database side feels like a mausoleum of data — dead, static, one-path.

**RIGHT HALF (the truth — attention mechanism as roundtable weighted aggregation):**
A warm, glowing round table seen from slightly above. Around it float 4-6 luminous nodes (representing tokens) connected by beams of light. The light beams have VISIBLY DIFFERENT THICKNESS AND BRIGHTNESS — some are thick and brilliant (weight 0.7), others thin and subtle (weight 0.2, 0.1). The connections form a web of varying intensity, creating a sense of fluid, continuous information flow. Warm amber/golden glow emanates from the connected nodes and spreads across the table surface. Small Chinese characters (猫, 追, 鼠) faintly visible inside or near the nodes as subtle labels.

**Transition between halves:**
A diagonal energy gradient from cold blue (left) to warm amber (right). The database side is visually "losing" — dimmer, with the red X dominating. The roundtable side is "winning" — brighter, more alive, drawing the eye.

**Lighting & atmosphere:**
- Volumetric light rays emanating from the right side nodes
- Subtle particle/dust motes floating in the warm light
- The red X on the left side casts a faint red glow
- Overall cinematic lighting with dramatic contrast between the cold and warm zones

**Title placement:**
Bottom third, centered, spanning both halves:
- "注意力机制是什么？" in large bold white Chinese characters
- "别再当数据库查询" below in slightly smaller size, with the words "数据库查询" in a subtle struck-through or dimmed style to echo the red X visual above

**Style reference:** High-end tech editorial illustration, like MIT Technology Review or Nature cover art. Clean, sophisticated, no cartoonish elements. No realistic human faces. The image should feel premium and intellectual.

**MUST avoid:**
- No cartoon or clip-art style
- No realistic human faces or figures
- No LaTeX or mathematical formulas in the image
- No English text (Chinese only)
- Text must be crisp and readable at thumbnail size
