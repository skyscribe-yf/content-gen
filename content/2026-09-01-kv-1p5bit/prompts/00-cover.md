# 封面图 Prompt (21:9)

## 标题
KV缓存压到1.5bit：省90%显存，凭什么不掉点？

## Prompt
A minimalist conceptual illustration, ultra-wide 21:9 composition. A tall pile of colorful sticky notes (representing KV cache) on the left is being squeezed into a thin, compact slice by two flat pressing panels. From the thin slice, a soft magnifying glass lens focuses a beam of light onto two small glyphs "K" and "V" on the right — the K glyph is sharp and crystallized, while the V glyph appears soft and blurred. Deep blue (#0F4C81) and white palette, clean white background with subtle grid, flat modern tech aesthetic, generous negative space across the wide frame. No numeric text, no sentences, only the two letters K and V.
