# 00-cover: 位置编码 封面图

## 设计

- Type: conceptual
- Palette: cool (深蓝 + 青色渐变)
- Rendering: flat-vector
- Text: title-only
- Mood: balanced
- Aspect: 2.35:1

## Prompt

A cinematic 2.35:1 flat-vector illustration for a Chinese tech article about "position encoding" in AI models.

Visual concept: The phrase "猫追老鼠" (cat chases mouse) appears twice on the canvas — once at the top with "猫" on the left and "老鼠" on the right connected by an arrow going right (→), and once at the bottom with positions swapped — "老鼠" on the left and "猫" on the right with arrow reversed (←). Between them, a glowing rotating vector diagram (a circle with an arrow rotating, labeled with angle θ) shows how position encoding captures word order through rotation. The background is a deep navy blue (#0a1628) with subtle cyan geometric grid lines. Minimalist, clean composition with 50% negative space.

Color palette: deep navy background, cyan (#00d4ff) and electric blue (#4488ff) for vector elements, white (#ffffff) for Chinese text.

Title text centered at bottom: "词序搞错意思全变！" in large bold sans-serif Chinese font, white color, with a thin cyan underline accent.

Secondary text below: "位置编码是怎么工作的" in smaller clean sans-serif, light gray (#aabbcc).

NO realistic humans. NO photorealistic elements. Flat vector style only. NO English text except the mathematical θ symbol.
