## ZONES

Square conceptual illustration with one visual focus: a bright jagged mountain ridge or roller-coaster path representing uneven intelligence. Several peaks glow with precise geometric structures, while nearby valleys dissolve into soft noise and broken puzzle pieces. The path should clearly feel continuous but uneven, with a strong diagonal movement from lower left to upper right.

## LABELS

No text, no numbers, no letters, no logos, no formulas. Convey the idea entirely through the jagged path, contrasting bright peaks, and sudden gaps.

## COLORS

Clean warm-white background, deep blue primary shapes, saturated golden yellow peaks, small vermilion warning accents, restrained cyan glow. High contrast and a clear silhouette at thumbnail size.

## STYLE

Editorial flat-vector technology illustration, refined geometric shapes, subtle grain, generous whitespace, visually simple but emotionally surprising. No literal human faces and no busy dashboard.

## ASPECT

1:1 square composition. Keep the main ridge and all important shapes away from the edges.
