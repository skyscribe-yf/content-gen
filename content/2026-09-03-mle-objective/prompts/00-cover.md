## ZONES

Ultra-wide cinematic composition. One single visual focus: a luminous neural-network prism splitting into two sharply contrasting outputs. On the left, a precise stream of clean code-like glyph blocks and geometric proof marks; on the right, a small messy childlike crayon path that suddenly breaks apart. The contrast should feel surprising and energetic, not like a textbook infographic. Keep all important objects away from the top and bottom edges. Put the title in the central-right negative space.

## LABELS

必须在画面中央偏右以清晰、准确、无错别字的简体中文排版 2 行标题：
第一行「MLE 博士生？」MLE 与“博士生”之间只使用一个空格，不要使用任何冒号。
第二行「小学生？」
第二行末尾必须使用中文全角问号「？」，绝不能使用半角问号「?」。只出现以上中文文字、字母和标点；不要出现英文说明、公式、数字、额外标签或乱码。标题文字必须清晰可读，字号足够大，适合缩小到信息流卡片后阅读。

## COLORS

Deep navy blue and white background, high-contrast warm yellow and orange accent, a small electric cyan highlight. Strong contrast between the two outputs. Avoid gray-on-gray and avoid many small colored icons.

## STYLE

Premium flat-vector editorial technology illustration, clean geometric shapes, subtle paper texture, crisp silhouette, bold visual tension, generous negative space, no logos, no watermark, no photorealistic people.

## ASPECT

21:9 ultra-wide cover. Main visual and title must remain inside the central safe area for possible crop. Title occupies a large readable area but does not span the entire width.
