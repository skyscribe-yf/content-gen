温度参数对 softmax 分布形状的影响

Minimalist hand-drawn line art style on warm cream (#FFFAF0) background. Intentional wobble on all lines. Generous white space. Chinese text labels, large and prominent with handwritten-style fonts.

LAYOUT: Three-panel horizontal comparison, left to right.

PANEL 1 - "T=0.1 晶体":
- Single sharp peak (almost vertical line) on a distribution curve
- Almost all probability concentrated on one class
- Physical icon: crystal/diamond shape (rigid, ordered)
- Label: "梯度集中在1-2个类"

PANEL 2 - "T=1.0 液体" (highlighted/central):
- Moderate bell-curve-like distribution with clear peak but some spread
- Physical icon: water drop (flowing, adaptable)
- Label: "梯度均衡，最佳平衡"
- Subtle border highlight indicating this is the default

PANEL 3 - "T=5.0 气体":
- Nearly flat/uniform distribution across all classes
- Physical icon: scattered dots/steam (free, dispersed)
- Label: "梯度均匀但被稀释"

BOTTOM TAGLINE: Hand-lettered: "低温冻住梯度，高温稀释梯度，T=1 刚刚好"

COLORS: Cream background (#FFFAF0), Warm Orange (#ED8936) for the T=1.0 panel highlight, Terracotta (#C05621) for T=0.1, Golden Yellow (#F6AD55) for T=5.0, Near Black (#1A1A1A) for all lines and text. Color accents under 15%.

STYLE: Clean composition with generous white space. Simple or no background. Hand-drawn wobble on all lines and curves. Chinese labels only. No computer fonts, no gradients, no shadows. Distribution curves hand-drawn, not mathematically precise.

ASPECT: 16:9, medium complexity
