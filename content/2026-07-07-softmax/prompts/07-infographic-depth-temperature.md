深度 × 温度 核心规律 - 梯度流动示意图

Minimalist hand-drawn line art style on warm cream (#FFFAF0) background. Intentional wobble on all lines. Generous white space. Chinese text labels, large and prominent with handwritten-style fonts.

LAYOUT: Top-to-bottom gradient flow diagram with three parallel paths.

THREE PARALLEL COLUMNS (left to right: T=0.1, T=1.0, T=5.0):

Each column shows a vertical stack of 6 layers (rectangles) with arrows between them representing gradient flow.

COLUMN 1 - "T=0.1":
- Top layer: thick arrow going down (output gradient amplified)
- Middle layers: arrows getting progressively thinner
- Bottom layer: almost invisible/dashed arrow (gradient vanished)
- Annotation: "梯度逐层消失"
- Red ❌ mark

COLUMN 2 - "T=1.0":
- Top layer: moderate arrow
- Middle layers: arrows remain visible, slightly thinner
- Bottom layer: still visible arrow
- Annotation: "梯度稳定传递"
- Green ✅ mark

COLUMN 3 - "T=5.0":
- Top layer: thin arrow (gradient diluted by /T)
- Middle layers: arrows consistently thin but present
- Bottom layer: thin but visible
- Annotation: "梯度均匀但弱"
- Yellow ⚠️ mark

BOTTOM: Horizontal bracket connecting all three columns with label: "网络越深，温度选择越关键"

COLORS: Cream background (#FFFAF0), Warm Orange (#ED8936) for T=1.0 column arrows, Soft Red (#E53E3E) for T=0.1 vanished arrows, Golden Yellow (#F6AD55) for T=5.0 thin arrows, Near Black (#1A1A1A) for all lines, layer boxes, and text. Color accents under 15%.

STYLE: Clean composition with generous white space. Simple or no background. Hand-drawn wobble on all lines and arrows. Chinese labels only. No computer fonts, no gradients, no shadows. Arrow thickness visually represents gradient magnitude.

ASPECT: 16:9, medium complexity
