Softmax vs Argmax 投票方式对比 - 比例代表制

Minimalist hand-drawn line art style on warm cream (#FFFAF0) background. Intentional wobble on all lines. Generous white space. Chinese text labels, large and prominent with handwritten-style fonts.

LAYOUT: Left | Right split comparison with hand-drawn vertical divider.

LEFT SIDE - "Argmax 独裁":
- One stick figure with a crown/trophy, raising hand triumphantly
- Score labels: A=0, B=0, C=1 (winner takes all)
- A red cross (❌) on the zero-score figures indicating "梯度=0, 没有入口"
- Small annotation: "只看第一，其余归零"

RIGHT SIDE - "Softmax 比例代表":
- Three stick figures each holding a proportional bar
- Score labels: A=2.7%, B=0.2%, C=97.1%
- A green check (✅) on the small percentages indicating "残留=梯度入口"
- Small annotation: "每个人都有份额"

BOTTOM: Hand-lettered tagline: "argmax 原地不动，softmax 留了缝隙"

COLORS: Cream background (#FFFAF0), Warm Orange (#ED8936) for softmax side accents, Terracotta (#C05621) for argmax side, Near Black (#1A1A1A) for all lines and text. Color accents under 10% of canvas.

STYLE: Clean composition with generous white space. Simple or no background. Main elements centered. Hand-drawn wobble on all lines. Chinese labels only. No computer fonts, no gradients, no shadows.

ASPECT: 16:9, medium complexity
