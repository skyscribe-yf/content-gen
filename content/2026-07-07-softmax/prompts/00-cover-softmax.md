Cinematic cover image for a Chinese technical article about Softmax function.

Concept: A transformation scene showing rigid, frozen arrows (argmax) melting into flowing, warm probability streams (softmax). The visual metaphor: temperature transforms rigid certainty into fluid probability.

COMPOSITION:
- Left third: Sharp, angular, ice-blue/crystalline shapes representing argmax — rigid, frozen, binary (0 or 1). Thin arrows pointing to a single peak.
- Center: A warm gradient transition zone where ice melts into flowing liquid. The temperature symbol "T" appears here as a glowing warm element.
- Right two-thirds: Warm, flowing, organic probability curves in warm orange/amber tones. Multiple soft probability streams flowing from a central distribution curve, each representing a different class getting its share.

The distribution curve on the right shows 3 soft peaks of different heights (97.1%, 2.7%, 0.2%) — the signature softmax output.

TITLE: "Softmax为什么不直接取最大值？" — displayed in large handwritten Chinese characters, positioned in the upper portion of the image, slightly offset left. Color: warm charcoal #4A4A4A or dark brown #744210.

PALETTE: warm — cream, warm orange, terracotta, golden yellow
- Background: Deep warm cream to soft amber gradient
- Left cold zone: Icy blue-white (#D4E6F1) fading to neutral
- Right warm zone: Warm Orange (#ED8936), Golden Yellow (#F6AD55), Terracotta (#C05621)
- Text: Warm Charcoal (#4A4A4A)

RENDERING: Hand-drawn aesthetic with slight wobble on lines. Soft edges, no harsh geometry. The probability curves should feel organic and fluid. The ice/crystal elements on the left should feel rigid and sharp by contrast.

MOOD: Balanced — the warmth of the right side should feel inviting and correct, while the cold left side feels restrictive and wrong. Not dramatic, but clearly showing the transformation.

Clean composition with 40-60% whitespace. No realistic humans. Simplified geometric and organic shapes only.

ASPECT: 2.35:1 (cinematic widescreen cover)
