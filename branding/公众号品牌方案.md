## 知乎头像

**文件**：`branding/zhihu-avatar-v2-1.png`（1024×1024）

**设计方向**：
- 核心图形：保留公众号大脑图标结构（左神经网 + 右数学符号）
- 配色：深蓝底 + 金色数学符号（替代粉橙渐变，更专业克制）
- 文字：不放文字，纯图标（知乎自动显示用户名）
- 风格：专业可信，适配知乎社区理性气质

**生成提示词**：
```
A professional square avatar icon. Background: rich, clearly visible deep royal blue (#1a3a6e to #0d2240 gradient), NOT black — the blue must be unmistakable. Center: a stylized brain icon split vertically into two halves, with a thin silver-white outline. Left half: neural network pattern with interconnected glowing nodes and lines in bright cyan (#00d4ff), clean geometric circular nodes of varying sizes connected by thin luminous lines. Right half: three elegant mathematical symbols stacked vertically — nabla (∇), sigma (Σ), and theta (θ) — rendered in bright metallic gold (#ffd700) with a warm golden glow. Small golden decorative dots scattered around the brain perimeter. No text, no letters, no numbers. Clean, professional, academic style. Centered composition with generous padding so the brain doesn't touch edges. The blue background must read as distinctly blue even at small sizes.
```

**生成参数**：`size=1:1 (1024x1024)`, `quality=high`, `model=gpt-image-2 (zairouter)`

## 知乎个人主页封面图

**文件**：`branding/zhihu-profile-cover.png`（1920×460）

**知乎规格**：宽 1920px × 高 460px（比例约 4.17:1），最低要求宽>1200px、高>240px

**设计方向**：
- 与知乎头像（大脑图标）视觉统一：深蓝底 + 青色神经网 + 金色数学符号
- 核心元素集中在中间窄条区域（上下会被知乎裁剪显示）
- 右侧展示三层拆解框架：为什么 → 怎么做 → 谁做得好
- 散布淡色数学公式和节点纹理作为装饰
- 风格：专业、克制、理性，适配知乎社区气质

**生成提示词**：
```
Ultra-wide horizontal banner, aspect ratio approximately 4:1 (much wider than tall). A professional Zhihu profile cover image. IMPORTANT: all key visual elements must be concentrated in a narrow horizontal band across the center of the image, with generous empty space at top and bottom that can be safely cropped away. Background: smooth deep royal blue gradient (#0d2240 to #1a3a6e). Center band content: a stylized brain icon split vertically — left half with cyan (#00d4ff) glowing neural network nodes and thin connection lines, right half with golden (#ffd700) mathematical symbols (nabla ∇, sigma Σ, theta θ). To the right of the brain, three small horizontal layers connected by thin lines: top "为什么", middle "怎么做", bottom "谁做得好" — in clean small white text. Far right edge: subtle small benchmark bar chart icon and code brackets in muted gold. Scattered across the full width: very faint translucent mathematical formulas and node patterns as decorative background texture. Style: clean digital illustration, professional, intellectual, minimalist. No people, no photorealism, no 3D. All text in Chinese where shown. The image must be extremely wide relative to its height.
```

**生成参数**：`size=21:9-4k (3840×1648)`, `quality=high`, `model=gpt-image-2 (zairouter)`，生成后中心裁剪至 1920×460
